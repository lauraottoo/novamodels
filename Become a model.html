TIPS TO GETTING SCOUTED

Professional model scouts are looking for something different than you might expect. We don't want to see you in your fanciest outfit with lots of makeup on. We are looking for you at your most natural. Have a clean face with absolutely no makeup. Pull your hair back. Wear a form fitting outfit like skinny jeans and a tank top. We need to see the shape of your body.

RECRUITMENT WARRING

Please be aware that there are individuals on the internet falsely claiming to be representatives of NOVA Models. Please note the following: You should never pay to attend a casting. You should never share photos in the nude or in lingerie. All NOVA Models representatives correspond via email domains that end with novamodels.com. If you are contacted by anyone claiming to be a representative of NOVA Models, do not respond without first verifying their identity by promptly calling us directly and we'll be happy to assist you. Please visit www.novamodels.com/contact for more information. You should always verify the identity of individuals who host castings through the NOVA Models casting platform.
